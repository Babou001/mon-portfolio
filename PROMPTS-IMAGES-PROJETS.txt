CONSEILS DE GENERATION
  - Genere en anglais : tous les modeles d'image sont bien meilleurs en anglais.
  - N'ECRIS JAMAIS DE TEXTE dans l'image. Les modeles produisent du faux texte
    illisible, et ca fait tout de suite amateur sur un portfolio. Les prompts
    ci-dessous contiennent deja "no text, no letters, no numbers".
  - Garde la meme seed / le meme style entre les 7 images. La coherence visuelle
    de la serie compte plus que la beaute de chaque image prise isolement.
  - Les images seront affichees en haut des cartes, en assez petit (~500px de
    large sur desktop). Evite les scenes chargees de details fins : privilegie
    une composition simple, lisible en miniature.
  - Prevois que le bas de l'image sera legerement assombri par un degrade vers
    le fond du site. Ne mets rien d'important dans le tiers inferieur.

[1] rag-idemia.webp
Systeme de recherche de documents avec chatbot RAG integre — IDEMIA France SAS
Tags : GenIA, Retrieval Augmented Generation — 2025
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
A vast dark archive of floating translucent document pages suspended in deep
space, arranged in a curved wall. Thin glowing cyan threads shoot out from a
single bright focal point and connect to a handful of specific pages, as if
retrieving them from the mass. The selected pages glow brighter, edged in violet.
Faint particles drift between them. Sense of instant search through an enormous
corpus.

INTENTION: la recherche documentaire massive + la recuperation ciblee (le "R" de
RAG). C'est ton projet phare en entreprise, soigne celui-la.

[2] rag-chat-system.webp
RAG Chat System — Application Full-Stack de Chat Intelligent — Projet personnel
Tags : GenIA, RAG, Full-Stack — 2025
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
Two glowing abstract panels facing each other in dark space: on the left a stack
of layered translucent document sheets emitting cyan light, on the right a
vertical flow of luminous green data streaming upward like a live response being
generated token by token. A bright violet arc bridges the two panels. Vector
embedding points scattered as a soft constellation in the background.

INTENTION: le dialogue documents <-> reponse streamee, et le cote full-stack
(deux moities reliees).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[3] attitudes-sociales.webp
Classification des attitudes sociales a partir de donnees temporelles
LIS-Lab – CNRS
Tags : Recherche, Interpretabilite — 2024
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
Abstract human silhouettes rendered only as constellations of glowing facial
landmark points and motion-tracking lines, two figures facing each other in
confrontation, no visible skin or features. Overlapping waveform ribbons in cyan
and violet flow between them along a horizontal time axis. Warm red-orange glow
on one figure, cold blue glow on the other, suggesting opposite emotional states.
Scientific, analytical, restrained.

INTENTION: l'analyse comportementale multimodale + l'axe temporel. Le contraste
chaud/froid renvoie directement a "colere chaude / colere froide".
ATTENTION: insiste sur "no visible face, only tracking points" — sinon le modele
va generer de vrais visages et ca jurera avec le reste de la serie.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[4] clustering-toyota.webp
Classification non supervisee des modeles Toyota — Euranova (Hackathon)
Tags : Hackathon, Clustering — 2023
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
A dark three-dimensional scatter plot floating in space, hundreds of small
luminous points naturally grouping into four or five distinct clusters. Each
cluster glows a different color: cyan, violet, tech green, soft pink. Faint
translucent hulls wrap each group. A few unassigned points drift in the empty
space between clusters. Elegant data visualization, generous negative space.

INTENTION: le clustering non supervise / la projection t-SNE. Composition la plus
simple des 7, elle sera tres lisible en miniature.
NOTE: pas de voiture, pas de logo Toyota (marque deposee). On reste sur l'abstrait.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[5] tours-de-parole.webp
Detection multimodale des tours de parole — Projet personnel
Tags : Multimodal, Speech Segmentation — 2024
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
A long horizontal audio waveform stretching across dark space, glowing cyan.
The waveform is cleanly cut into alternating colored segments — cyan blocks and
violet blocks — separated by sharp vertical light beams marking the boundaries.
Above the waveform, a parallel ribbon of abstract flowing text-like particles
(no readable characters) runs in sync. Two distinct luminous sources at each end
of the axis, suggesting two speakers alternating.

INTENTION: la segmentation en tours de parole + la fusion audio/texte.
ATTENTION: le ruban "text-like" doit rester des particules abstraites, jamais des
lettres lisibles.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[6] agent-dataframes.webp
Agent IA d'analyse de DataFrames — LangChain / Pandas (en cours)
Tags : Agent IA, DataFrames — 2025
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
A glowing central orb of violet light floating above a dark translucent grid of
tabular data. Three luminous branches radiate from the orb toward three distinct
abstract tools: a magnifying lens of light, a bar chart made of glowing bars, and
a flowing stream of green data. The grid below reacts, some rows lighting up in
cyan. Sense of an intelligent router dispatching a request to the right tool.

INTENTION: le tool-routing de l'agent (query_df / describe_df / plot_df) au-dessus
d'un DataFrame. C'est le seul projet "en cours", l'image peut etre un peu plus
aerienne.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[7] yolov8-dechets.webp
Detection de dechets par YOLOv8 — Projet personnel
Tags : Computer Vision, YOLOv8 — 2024
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PROMPT:
Dark scene with scattered abstract waste objects — bottles, cans, wrappers —
rendered as low-poly luminous shapes. Each object is precisely enclosed in a
thin glowing rectangular detection box, cyan and green, with small bright corner
brackets. Faint scan lines sweep across the scene. Clean computer-vision
inference look, technical and precise.

INTENTION: la detection d'objets avec bounding boxes, immediatement reconnaissable.
ATTENTION: pas de score de confiance affiche au-dessus des boites (ce serait du
texte). Juste les cadres.
